## Core Qualifications Grounding (2024 V1.1)

### 1. Commercial Intelligence (CI-L6-2024)
- **Credits**: 10 Credits | 100 TQT | 80 GLH
- **Assessment**: Onscreen Multiple-Choice Test, 45 Questions, 90 Minutes
- **Target Grade**: Distinction (80%+)
- **Syllabus Pillars**:
  - **LO1**: Performance Metrics & Financial Contribution (ROMI, CAC, CLV, Margin, CVP Break-Even, SOV, Bi-variant correlation, A/B Testing & Attribution).
  - **LO2**: Marketing Resource Management (5Ms: Men, Money, Materials, Minutes, Measurements, MarTech Stack, Resource Audits).
  - **LO3**: Forecasting & Strategic Budgeting (Zero-Based, Activity-Based, Flexible Budgeting, Time Series, Moving Averages, Variance Analysis, Business Cases).

### 2. Strategy and Planning (SP-L6-2024) — Featured Case: Nyika.app
- **Credits**: 20 Credits | 200 TQT | 160 GLH
- **Assessment**: Onscreen Plan Submission + Short & Extended Answer Test (2 Hours)
- **Core Case Study**: **Nyika.app Mobility & Logistics** — Scaling motorcycle fare bidding and B2B express parcel courier across Southern Africa (Harare, Johannesburg, Lusaka, Maputo).
- **Target Grade**: Distinction (80%+)
- **Syllabus Pillars**:
  - **LO1**: Mission, Vision & Strategic Alignment (PESTEL: currency, mobile money, urban traffic; Objective Cascades).
  - **LO2**: Strategic Audits (Porter's 5 Forces, 7Ps, Ansoff, BCG, Mendelow, TOWS).
  - **LO3**: Strategy Formulation (SFA Criteria, STP, SMART Objectives).
  - **LO4**: Aligning Supporting Plans (SOSTAC®, 7Ps Marketing Mix for B2B Cargo).
  - **LO5**: Implementation & Governance (5Ms: Men, Money, Materials, Minutes, Measurements; Gantt Milestones).
  - **LO6**: Control & Continuous Improvement (Marketing Dashboards, PDCA Cycle).
