CIM Level 6 Commercial Intelligence

Official 2024 V1.1 Formula Reference

1. Return on Marketing Investment (ROMI) LO 1.1

ROMI (%) = [ ( (Gross Revenue × Gross Margin %) - Marketing Spend ) / Marketing Spend ] × 100
Example: Revenue £320k, Margin 40%, Spend £80k.
Gross Profit = £128k. Net Marketing Profit = £128k - £80k = £48k.
ROMI = (£48k / £80k) × 100 = 60.0%

2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) LO 1.1 & 2.1

CAC (£) = Total Marketing & Sales Costs in Period / Total New Customers Acquired
Benchmarking: Healthy businesses maintain an LTV : CAC ratio of 3:1 or higher.

3. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV / LTV) LO 1.1

Average Lifespan = 1 / Churn Rate %
CLV (£) = (Average Revenue per Period × Gross Margin %) / Churn Rate %
Example: £50/mo subscription, 80% margin, 4% churn.
Lifespan = 1 / 0.04 = 25 months. Monthly Margin = £40.
CLV = £40 × 25 = £1,000

4. Break-Even Volume & Revenue LO 1.1

Unit Contribution Margin = Selling Price - Variable Cost per Unit
Break-Even Units = Fixed Marketing Overheads / Unit Contribution Margin
Example: Overheads £60,000. Price £120, Variable Cost £70 (Contribution = £50).
Break-Even Units = £60,000 / £50 = 1,200 units (£144,000 revenue).

5. Net Promoter Score (NPS) LO 2.1

NPS = % Promoters (Scores 9-10) - % Detractors (Scores 0-6)
Note: Passives (Scores 7-8) are excluded from the net score. NPS ranges between -100 and +100.

6. Moving Average Seasonal Forecast LO 3.2

Seasonally Adjusted Forecast = 4-Quarter Trend Value × Seasonal Index
Example: Trend estimate £450k, Q4 Seasonal Index 1.25 -> Forecast = £562,500.
