CIM Level 6 Strategic Frameworks Reference

Essential models for Strategy & Planning (SP-L6-2024)

1. SOSTAC® Planning Framework

• Situation (Where are we now?)
• Objectives (Where do we want to be?)
• Strategy (How do we get there?)
• Tactics (The details of strategy - 7Ps)
• Action (The 5Ms, Gantt schedules, Who does what?)
• Control (KPIs, Dashboards, PDCA cycle)
Application: Primary planning backbone for marketing plan submissions.

2. SFA Matrix (Johnson, Scholes & Whittington)

• Suitability (Does it address audit findings & strategic drift?)
• Feasibility (Can we deliver with available 5Ms & financial cash?)
• Acceptability (Does the risk-reward & ROMI satisfy stakeholders?)
Application: Essential for LO 3.2 to justify why an option was chosen over alternatives.

3. TOWS Matrix (Strategic Synthesis)

• SO (Maxi-Maxi): Use internal strengths to exploit external opportunities.
• ST (Maxi-Mini): Use strengths to avoid/mitigate external threats.
• WO (Mini-Maxi): Overcome internal weaknesses by taking opportunities.
• WT (Mini-Mini): Defensive options to minimize weaknesses and threats.
Application: Translates audit data into concrete strategic options.

4. The 5Ms Resource Audit

• Men (Human capital, talent, skills, capacity)
• Money (Capex, Opex, working capital, budget flexibility)
• Materials (MarTech stack, physical assets, creative collateral)
• Minutes (Time to market, campaign runway, schedule deadlines)
• Measurements (KPIs, analytics maturity, control ratios)
Application: Evaluates internal feasibility under LO 2.1 & LO 5.1.
