CIM Level 6 Command Verbs Master Table

From official CIM Qualifications Specification 2024 V1.1 (Pages 14-17)

Command Verb Standard Definition Level 6 Cognitive Expectation
Analyse Examine a topic together with thoughts and judgements about it. Analyse new/abstract data and schools of thought; consider alternative solutions and outcomes independently using models and definitions. Compare models using appropriate criteria.
Appraise Evaluate, judge or assess. Provide a comprehensive and detailed critique of the subject area demonstrating in-depth understanding and awareness.
Argue Provide reasoned arguments for or against a point to reach a conclusion. Engage in debate in a professional manner evidencing comprehensive understanding and application of key principles.
Assess Evaluate or judge the importance of something referring to schools of thought. Synthesise and assess new and/or abstract information and data in the context of a broad range of problems using a range of techniques.
Critically Evaluate Form a well-informed judgement taking into account multiple perspectives. Assess value/quality in a thorough, analytical manner. Examine strengths/weaknesses, alternative viewpoints, and question assumptions to reach a balanced conclusion.
Determine Use research/calculation to check or establish something. Execute a defined project of research, development or calculation to identify evidence. Make judgements where data is limited.
Justify Support recommendations or arguments with valid reasons for and against. Communicate well-structured and coherent arguments relevant to marketing objectives and financial feasibility.
Recommend Put forward proposals supported by a clear rationale. Produce reliable and valid conclusions and proposals based on abstract data and situation, appropriately contextualised.
