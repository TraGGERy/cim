# CIM Level 6 High-Trust Primary Resources

## Primary Syllabus Documents
1. **CIM Level 6 Award in Commercial Intelligence 2024 (Module Specification) V1.1**
   - *Ofqual Regulated Provider / European Marketing Confederation Accredited*
   - Assessment: 45 Multiple-Choice Questions, 90 Minutes, Onscreen.
   - Grading: Distinction (80%+), Merit (70-79%), Pass (60-69%), Fail (0-59%).
2. **CIM Level 6 Award in Strategy and Planning 2024 (Module Specification) V1.1**
   - Assessment: Onscreen Plan Submission + Short and Extended Answer Response Test, 2 Hours.
   - Credits: 20 Credits (200 TQT, 160 GLH).

## Authoritative Marketing & Financial Literature
- **Marketing Metrics & Financial Analysis**: Farris, Bendle, Pfeifer, & Reibstein (2021) *Marketing Metrics: The Manager's Guide to Measuring Marketing Performance*.
- **The Long and the Short of It / ESOV Principles**: Binet & Field (IPA Databank Empirical Research).
- **Strategic Planning Frameworks**:
  - PR Smith's SOSTAC® Planning Model.
  - Johnson, Scholes & Whittington's Exploring Strategy (SFA: Suitability, Feasibility, Acceptability).
  - Ansoff's Growth Matrix; Boston Consulting Group (BCG) Growth-Share Matrix.
  - Mendelow's Power/Interest Stakeholder Matrix.
- **Resource & Budgeting Principles**: Kaplan & Norton's Balanced Scorecard; CIMA Official Terminology for Zero-Based, Activity-Based, and Flexible Budgeting.
